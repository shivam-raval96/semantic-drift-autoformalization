Read the story and the candidate formalization, then judge the candidate.

A formalization writes the story as two lines: "ASSUME:" for the story's standing habit and "ASK:" for its question, using op(a, b) for the combining action applied to a first and a second input, and letters for the arbitrary starting items. Judge it correct only if both lines match the story exactly, including the order of inputs.
